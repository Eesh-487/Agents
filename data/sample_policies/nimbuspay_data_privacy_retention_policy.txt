NimbusPay Technologies Pvt Ltd
Data Privacy and Retention Policy
Document ID: NPT-POL-DP-01 | Version 2.1 | Effective Date: 2025-01-15

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This policy governs how NimbusPay Technologies Pvt Ltd ("NimbusPay"), a digital
lending and payments platform operating in India, collects, processes, stores,
and retains personal data belonging to customers, loan applicants, and
platform users. It applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party
vendors who process personal data on NimbusPay's behalf.

2. DEFINITIONS

"Personal Data" means any data about an individual who is identifiable by or
in relation to such data, including name, contact details, financial
information, KYC documents, and transaction history.

"Data Principal" means the individual to whom the personal data relates.

"Processing" means any operation performed on personal data, including
collection, storage, use, disclosure, or erasure.

3. DATA COLLECTION PRINCIPLES

3.1 NimbusPay collects only the personal data necessary to provide lending
and payment services, including KYC verification, credit assessment, and
transaction processing.

3.2 The Product Engineering department is responsible for ensuring that data
collection forms and APIs do not request data beyond what is specified in
this policy.

3.3 The Compliance department owns the Consent Management Control, which
governs how and when consent is collected from Data Principals prior to
processing their personal data.

4. DATA RETENTION

4.1 Customer KYC documents and transaction records are retained for a period
of 8 years from the date of account closure, in line with RBI recordkeeping
norms for regulated lending entities.

4.2 Marketing and analytics data collected through the mobile application is
retained for 24 months from the date of collection, after which it is
anonymized.

4.3 The Data Engineering department owns the Data Retention Control, which is
implemented through automated deletion jobs running on the customer data
warehouse.

5. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

5.1 Data Principals may request access to, correction of, or erasure of their
personal data by submitting a request through the NimbusPay support portal.

5.2 The Customer Support department is responsible for triaging and
forwarding data subject requests to the Compliance department within 3
business days.

5.3 The Compliance department owns the Data Subject Request Handling
Process, which coordinates the response across Data Engineering, Product
Engineering, and Customer Support.

6. DATA SECURITY AND BREACH NOTIFICATION

6.1 The Information Security department owns the Data Encryption Control,
which mandates encryption of personal data at rest and in transit across all
NimbusPay systems.

6.2 In the event of a data breach involving personal data, the Information
Security department is responsible for initiating the Incident Response
Process and notifying the Compliance department within 24 hours of
detection.

6.3 NimbusPay does not currently maintain a documented process for notifying
affected Data Principals or regulators following a confirmed data breach.

7. THIRD-PARTY AND CROSS-BORDER DATA SHARING

7.1 Personal data may be shared with third-party credit bureaus and payment
processors strictly for the purposes described in Section 1.

7.2 The Vendor Management Control, owned by the Compliance department,
requires all third-party vendors handling personal data to sign a data
processing agreement prior to onboarding.

7.3 NimbusPay does not currently restrict which countries customer data may
be transferred to when using cloud infrastructure providers.

8. GOVERNANCE

8.1 The Compliance department is the designated owner of this policy and is
responsible for its periodic review, at minimum on an annual basis.

8.2 This policy does not currently designate a named Data Protection Officer
responsible for overseeing NimbusPay's compliance with applicable data
protection law.
